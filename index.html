What We Do

Services

Engineering support structured around how process challenges actually unfold — from concept through to operating reality.

Conceptual & Basic Engineering

  • Conceptual and feasibility studies
  • Basic engineering packages: PFDs, heat and mass balances, equipment datasheets, P&IDs and other deliverables
  • Simulation and optimization studies
  • Technology evaluation
  • Scale-up from laboratory and pilot-plant data
  • Debottlenecking of existing plants for higher capacity

Process Simulation & Modelling

  • Batch distillation column simulation
  • Continuous distillation simulation studies
  • Debottlenecking studies for existing column adequacy
  • Vendor coordination for column internals

Process Safety Studies

  • Safety valve sizing
  • Adequacy checks of existing safety valves for revamp conditions
  • Flare header sizing and adequacy checks
  • HAZOP studies

Heat Exchanger Design

  • Heat exchanger design and rating studies
  • Adequacy checks of existing heat exchangers
  • Air cooler design
  • Pinch analysis

Process PMC Services

  • Review of EPC contractors' process documents on the Owner's behalf
  • Bridging the gap between operations and engineering consultants
  • Participation in P&ID review, HAZID, HAZOP, model review and constructability workshops
  • Independent technical and quality review
  • Effective, timely communication with vendors
  • Support with vendor TQs and vendor document reviews
  • Faster document turnaround for the Owner and fewer late-stage changes
